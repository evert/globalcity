## SimCity / Micropolis

- SimCity was designed and written by **Will Wright** and first published by **Maxis** in 1989. The tile
  artwork is Maxis's original SimCity Classic art, as shipped in the Unix/X11 version of the game that
  **Don Hopkins** ported in the early 1990s.
- Maxis became part of Electronic Arts in 1997, so EA holds the copyright
  (Copyright (C) 1989 - 2007 Electronic Arts Inc.). In 2008 EA released the SimCity Classic source code
  and artwork under the GPLv3 for the One Laptop per Child project under the name **Micropolis**. That
  open-source release, and its continued maintenance, is the work of Don Hopkins (SimHacker):
  https://github.com/SimHacker/micropolis
- What GlobalCity uses from Micropolis: the tile artwork in `client/public/assets/micropolis/tiles.png`
  (16x16 px tiles, 960 tiles, laid out 16 columns by 60 rows, taken from
  `MicropolisCore/src/CellEngine/python/images/tiles.png` in that repository), and the tile numbering,
  flag bits, road/rail/wire connection tables and zone-population formulas in `shared/src/tiles.ts`.
  The moving-object sprites in `client/public/assets/micropolis/sprites.png` (layout in `sprites.json`) are
  the 61 frames `obj1-0.png` ... `obj8-3.png` from `MicropolisCore/src/images/micropolisEngine/` in that
  repository, packed one row per sprite type without modification. `shared/src/animation.ts` ports the
  tile animation table (`animate.cpp`) and the smoke/stadium/radar animation tables
  (`simulate.cpp`, `micropolis-activity/src/sim/s_zone.c`) from the same repository.
  The simulation itself is a simplified re-implementation inspired by the Micropolis sources; no
  Micropolis code is copied.

## GlobalCity additions to the tile sheet

- `client/public/assets/micropolis/tiles.png` is generated by `art/sheet/build_sheet.py`: the original
  960-tile Micropolis sheet (kept unmodified in `art/sheet/micropolis-tiles-original.png`) followed by
  GlobalCity's own tiles. Tiles 960-975 are the city hall, original pixel art drawn for GlobalCity in the
  Micropolis palette and style (`art/prototype/civic/city_hall.py`), released under the same GPL-3.0-or-later
  licence as the rest of GlobalCity.

## Natural Earth

- World coastlines, lakes, rivers, glaciers, the Antarctic ice shelves and the place names come from Natural Earth, which is in the public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com (fetched as GeoJSON from https://github.com/martynafford/natural-earth-geojson, CC0 1.0)

## Climate, forests, sea ice, elevation and plate boundaries

The world's biomes (desert, steppe, tundra, boreal forest, ice sheets), its forests and its frozen seas are derived from these datasets. The derived map (data/world-*/base.bin) is an adaptation of them.

- Climate zones: Beck, H. E., T. R. McVicar, N. Vergopolan, A. Berg, N. J. Lutsko, A. Dufour, Z. Zeng, X. Jiang, A. I. J. M. van Dijk, and D. G. Miralles. High-resolution (1 km) Köppen-Geiger maps for 1901–2099 based on constrained CMIP6 projections, Scientific Data 10, 724 (2023). 1991–2020 map, licensed CC BY 4.0: https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.21789074
- Tree cover: Hengl, T. (2024). Global tree percent cover and impervious surfaces (urban) cover based on GLC_FCS30D for years 1985 and 2022, 2022 tree cover at 1 km, licensed CC BY 4.0: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.14439377
- Sea ice: Fetterer, F., K. Knowles, W. N. Meier, M. Savoie, and A. K. Windnagel. Sea Ice Index, Version 4 (G02135), monthly extent 2015–2024. Boulder, Colorado USA. NSIDC: National Snow and Ice Data Center. https://doi.org/10.7265/a98x-0f50. A sea is frozen in GlobalCity where it had ice in at least half of those months.
- Elevation: NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information. 2022: ETOPO 2022 15 Arc-Second Global Relief Model (60 arc-second surface elevation). NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information. https://doi.org/10.25921/fd45-gt74 (public domain: CC0 1.0). Stored per tile in data/world-*/elevation.bin; floods only cover low land.
- Earthquake zones: Bird, P. (2003), An updated digital model of plate boundaries, Geochemistry, Geophysics, Geosystems 4(3), 1027, doi:10.1029/2001GC000252, as converted by the fraxen/tectonicplates project (GeoJSON by csterling; https://github.com/fraxen/tectonicplates), licensed under the Open Data Commons Attribution License 1.0.
