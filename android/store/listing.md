## Full description (max 4000 characters)

GlobalCity is a classic city builder on one giant map of the whole Earth, shared by everyone who plays.

Claim a plot anywhere on the planet, maybe right where you live, and grow it into a city. Zone homes, shops and industry, lay roads, rail and power lines, and watch your city come to life in the pixel art of the original SimCity, from the open-source Micropolis project.

You are never alone: other mayors build right next to you.

• Neighbours matter: nearby cities bring jobs and shoppers, and you can connect to them by rail.
• Trade power with neighbouring cities over your power lines.
• Planes fly between cities with airports, and ships sail between seaports.
• Unlock milestone buildings as your city grows: City Hall, schools, a university, stadiums, wind farms, airports, seaports and Grand Central station.
• Fight fires, keep crime down with police, ease traffic with rail, and keep pollution in check.
• Climb the leaderboard of the world's biggest cities.

Zoom out to see every city on Earth at once, or zoom in to watch the traffic and trains.

No accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases. Your city is waiting for you whenever you come back.

GlobalCity uses graphics from Micropolis, the open-source release of SimCity Classic, under the GPLv3 licence. It is not affiliated with Electronic Arts or the SimCity brand.

## Data safety form (suggested answers)

- **Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?** Yes, collected; none shared.
- **Is all of the user data encrypted in transit?** Yes (HTTPS and secure WebSockets).
- **Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted?** Yes (Discord or the developer email).
- **Data types collected:**
  - *Device or other IDs*: a random player ID the app creates, for **app functionality**. Required, not shared, not used for tracking.
  - *App activity → Other user-generated content*: the mayor and city names the player chooses and their city, for **app functionality**. Required, not shared.
- **Location:** not collected. "Use my location" is used on the device only, to move the map; the coordinates are never sent.
