Privacy policy

GlobalCity · last updated 28 October 2026

GlobalCity is a multiplayer city-building game on a map of the whole Earth, played at globalcity.evertpot.com and in the GlobalCity Android app (which opens the same website). This page explains what the game stores about you. In short: no accounts, no ads, no tracking.

What the game stores

  • A random player ID and key, created the first time you play and kept in your browser's local storage (or the app's), so you return to the same city. There is no account, email address or password.
  • The names you choose for your mayor and your city, and your city itself: the plots you claim, what you build, your funds, population and yearly budget. Mayor and city names, city locations and populations are visible to every player, for example on the leaderboard and the world map.
  • Where you are looking on the map, while you are online, so other players see a marker for you there. It is not stored after you leave.
  • Small settings in local storage, such as whether you have seen the welcome screen.

The game server keeps an operational log of game events (for example "a mayor connected" or "claimed a plot"), identified by mayor name and the start of the random player ID. It contains no IP addresses or device details.

Your location

If you choose "Use my location", your device's location is used only inside the game on your device, to move the map to where you are. Your coordinates are never sent to the server. If you then claim a plot there, the server stores that plot, which places your city within a few hundred kilometres of you, and other players can see where your city is.

Notifications

If you tap "Notify me" for the world reset, your browser (or, in the Android app, Chrome) gives the server a push address: a random web address at your browser's push service (for example Google's or Mozilla's) that lets us send that one notification, plus the encryption keys that come with it. The server stores it with the date you signed up and the kind of device (Android app, Android, iPhone, desktop) so we can count sign-ups, and nothing that links it to your mayor or city. Tap "Turn off notification" or block notifications for the site to stop it; addresses the push service reports as expired are deleted.

What the game does not do

  • No advertising, no analytics and no third-party trackers.
  • No sale or sharing of data with anyone.
  • The game server does not log IP addresses. The site is served through Cloudflare, which handles connections (and may briefly process IP addresses) to deliver and protect the site; see Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Keeping and deleting data

Cities stay in the shared world. A city whose mayor has not visited for 60 days is released and its plots can be claimed by others, together with everything built on them; a plot with fewer than five things built is released after an hour away. If you retire a city as a monument, the monument keeps the city's name and the mayor's name.

To have your city and player data removed sooner, ask in the GlobalCity Discord or through the contact on the Google Play listing and tell us your mayor and city name. Clearing the site's data in your browser (or the app's storage) removes your player key from your device.

Children

The game does not ask for personal information and is not directed at collecting data from children. Mayor and city names are public, so please do not use your real name.
