Names and public information

Mayor names, city names, city locations and populations are visible to every player. Choose names that are fine for everyone to see:

  • no hateful, harassing, sexual or violent names;
  • no pretending to be another player, the developer or a real person or organisation;
  • no advertising, links or personal information (yours or anyone else's), including your real name.

Names that break these rules may be changed or removed without notice.

Play fair

Please don't:

  • use bots, scripts or modified clients to play, or run many players to get around the limits on plots, money or lifelines;
  • deliberately exploit bugs. If you find one, please report it in the Discord;
  • try to break, overload or get unauthorised access to the game server or other players' cities;
  • harass other players.

A shared world

Your city is part of one world shared with everyone. A city whose mayor has not visited for 60 days is released, and its plots can be claimed by others, together with everything built on them; a plot with fewer than five things built is released after an hour away. A retired city stays on the map as a monument.

Cities, money and progress are kept on a best-effort basis. They can be lost, for example after a bug, a server problem or a reset of the world, and they cannot be restored or compensated. Your player key is kept only on your device. If you clear the site's data or the app's storage, you lose access to your city.
